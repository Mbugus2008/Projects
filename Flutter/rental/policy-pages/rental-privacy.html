This policy explains what personal data the Rental Management mobile app collects, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Rental Management Android app and the services it connects to.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: first name, last name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Property and tenancy records that you or your organisation enter: property and unit details, tenant names and contact details, lease terms, rent invoices and payment entries, maintenance records, and document records.

If you enter information about other people, such as tenants, you are responsible for making sure you have a lawful basis to do so and for letting them know how their data is used.

Information we collect automatically

  • Authentication token stored on your device so you stay signed in.
  • Basic technical and log data generated by our servers when you use the app, such as request timestamps and errors, used to operate and secure the service.

The app does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or files, and does not use advertising identifiers.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • Provide the property, tenant, lease, invoice, maintenance, document and reporting features of the app.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Keep the service secure, prevent misuse, and meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. Legal basis

We process your data to perform our contract with you, for our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, to comply with legal obligations, and on your consent where we ask for it.

5. How we store and protect your data

Your data is transmitted to our servers over HTTPS (TLS) and stored on servers we control, with access limited to authorised personnel. Your sign-in token is stored on your device using the operating system's encrypted secure storage. We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls and encryption in transit.

6. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who host, back up or support the service on our behalf, under contract and only on our instructions.
  • Authorities or other parties where we are required to do so by law.
  • Your organisation's administrators, where the account belongs to an organisation.

7. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. When it is no longer needed we delete it or anonymise it. See our account deletion page for how to request deletion.

8. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data deleted, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact info@trimline.co.ke. We will respond within the period required by law.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya at odpc.go.ke.

9. Children

The app is intended for business use by landlords, property managers and their staff. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. International transfers

We host your data on servers controlled by us. If data is transferred outside Kenya, we put appropriate safeguards in place as required by law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date above and, where the changes are significant, notify you in the app.
