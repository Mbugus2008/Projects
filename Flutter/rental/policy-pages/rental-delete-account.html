Rental Management — Delete your account

App com.trimline.rental · Effective 11 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Rental Management account and the data associated with it. Rental Management is operated by Trimline Systems & Solutions.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@trimline.co.ke from the email address registered to your Rental Management account, with the subject line “Rental Management account deletion request”.
  2. Include your account email address and, if your account belongs to an organisation, the organisation name.
  3. We will acknowledge your request and complete the deletion, normally within 30 days. If we need to verify your identity first, we will tell you.

Deleting your account is permanent. Once the request is processed you will no longer be able to sign in, and the deleted records cannot be recovered.

What we delete

  • Your account profile: first name, last name, email address, phone number and password.
  • The records you created or that belong to your account: properties and units, tenant contact details, leases, rent invoices and payment entries, maintenance records and document records.
  • Your stored sign-in token and any active sessions, which are removed from the app when you sign out or when the account is deleted.

What we keep, and for how long

We keep a limited set of records where we are required to do so by law, for example accounting, tax and audit records, and records needed to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud.

Retained dataReasonRetention period
Financial records (invoice and payment history) Tax and accounting obligations As required by Kenyan tax law, then deleted
Records subject to an active dispute or legal claim Legal defence Until the matter is closed, then deleted

Retained records are held securely and used only for the reasons above.

Delete some data without closing your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to have individual data removed. Email info@trimline.co.ke from your registered address and tell us which records you want deleted, for example a specific property, tenant, lease or document record. We will delete them, or explain why we must keep them, normally within 30 days.
